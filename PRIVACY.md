# NearChat Privacy Policy
NearChat does not collect, upload, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads or internet access.
Messages and files go directly between two phones over Bluetooth, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key that exists only in memory for that session.
Chats (text, file names and photo/video thumbnails) and files you accept are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage, so you can see past chats. You can delete any message, any chat, or all chats inside the app at any time; deleting a chat also removes the files received in it. Uninstalling the app removes everything.
Permissions: Nearby devices/Bluetooth (find and connect to phones), Location on Android 11 and below (required by Android for Bluetooth scanning; not stored or shared).
